Privacy policy

This policy explains what information we collect when you visit innerhellrecords.co.uk, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Inner Hell Records ("IHR", "we", "us", "our"), an independent extreme metal record label based at 128 City Road, London, United Kingdom, is the data controller responsible for your personal data.

Contact: innerhellrecords@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

We keep data collection to a minimum. Our website may collect the following:

  • Analytics is not currently active on this website. If it is switched on, it will collect aggregate usage data, such as pages visited and approximate location, only if you accept analytics cookies.
  • If you email us, we hold your name, email address and the content of your message only to respond to you.
  • We do not take payments on this website. Purchases are made on the third-party pages that this website links to, such as Bandcamp, and we do not receive or store your payment details.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you request a page, under GitHub's own privacy statement.
  • The Instagram feed on our home page is loaded from Behold when the page opens, so Behold may receive technical data such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy.
  • Your cookie choice is stored in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is not sent to us.

3. Legal basis for processing

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), we process personal data on the following lawful bases:

  • Consent applies to analytics cookies, which are not currently active. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interests apply to responding to your enquiries and operating this website.
  • Legal obligation applies where we are required to retain data by law.

4. Cookies and analytics

Analytics is not currently active on this website. If it is switched on, it will run only after you accept analytics cookies in the cookie banner, and this policy will be updated. If you deny them, no analytics cookies are set. You can clear your browser storage for this site and revisit it to change your choice.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. Data may be shared only with:

  • GitHub, which hosts this website under its own privacy statement.
  • Behold, which supplies the Instagram feed on our home page under its own privacy policy.
  • Legal authorities, if required by law.

Some pages also link to third-party services, including Bandcamp, Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Instagram and DistroKid Hyperfollow. When you follow an outbound link, that third party may collect data about you under its own privacy policy. We have no control over, and are not responsible for, those policies.

6. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected. Support correspondence is retained for up to 24 months after the last communication, unless a longer period is required by law.

7. International transfers

GitHub and Behold may process data outside the UK and the European Economic Area (EEA). Where they do, we rely on the safeguards they provide under UK GDPR and EU GDPR, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

8. Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you (access).
  • Request correction of inaccurate data (rectification).
  • Request deletion of your personal data (erasure, or the "right to be forgotten").
  • Request that we limit how we use your data (restriction).
  • Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format (portability).
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at innerhellrecords@gmail.com. We will respond within one month, as required by law.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's supervisory authority for data protection. If you are based in the EU, you may also contact your local data protection authority.

10. Children's privacy

Our website and services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us:

Email: innerhellrecords@gmail.com
