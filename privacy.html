Privacy Policy

Inner Hell Records ("IHR", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit innerhellrecords.co.uk, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who We Are

Inner Hell Records is an independent extreme metal record label based at 128 City Road, London, United Kingdom. You can contact us at innerhellrecords@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

This website does not require you to register or submit personal information to browse it. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information directly through this site.

If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you choose to provide (such as your name and email address) and will use it only to respond to your enquiry.

Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies and similar browser storage only with your explicit consent. When you first visit the site, a cookie consent banner appears allowing you to accept or deny non-essential cookies. Essential storage required for basic site functionality (for example, remembering your consent choice) does not require consent.

Analytics cookies: if accepted, we may use anonymised analytics to understand how visitors use the site (such as pages visited, referring URLs, and approximate location). This data is used solely to improve the website. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser storage for this site and revisiting it.

Local storage: we use your browser's local storage to remember your cookie consent preference. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Third-Party Services

Some pages embed third-party content (for example, our Instagram feed via Behold, and links to Bandcamp, Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, and Instagram). When you interact with embedded content or follow an outbound link, that third party may collect data about you under their own privacy policies. We have no control over, and are not responsible for, those policies.

Your Rights

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have rights regarding any personal data we hold about you, including the right to access, rectify, erase, and restrict processing. As we do not currently collect personal data beyond anonymised analytics (where consent is given), these rights are limited in scope on this site. To exercise any of your rights, please email innerhellrecords@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date below.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email innerhellrecords@gmail.com.
